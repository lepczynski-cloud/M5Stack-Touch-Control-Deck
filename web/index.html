WHAT IT DOES

Small device. Useful shortcuts.

One screen for the things you reach for every day. Swipe between pages and make it yours.

01

Your favorite apps

Six editable buttons for websites and everyday tools. Launch a URL with a single touch.

ChatGPT · Claude · Gemini

02

Media & volume

Play, pause, skip or mute. The optional Scroll Unit adds a physical volume knob.

03

Shortcuts for your OS

System monitor, screenshots, files, app switching and screen lock with OS-specific shortcuts.

04

Two time zones

See local time and another city. Synchronize over Wi-Fi and keep time using the RTC.

05

Bluetooth HID

Your computer recognizes a standard BLE keyboard. Select the OS after pairing, no companion app needed.

06

Local configuration

Change host OS, buttons, brightness and time zones through the local device portal.

WEB FLASHER

From browser to device.

No local compilation for visitors. Just the correct board, a USB data cable and a compatible desktop browser.

01

Connect your device

Plug your M5Stack CoreS3 SE into your computer with a USB-C data cable. The battery and Scroll Unit are optional.

02

Install in the browser

Choose Update to keep settings or Factory for a new device. Select your USB port.

03

Pair and choose your OS

Pair “M5 Control Deck” over Bluetooth, then choose Windows, macOS or Linux GNOME on the touchscreen.

i
Before you start

For CoreS3 SE only. To keep settings, select Firmware update and DO NOT select Erase device in the flashing dialog. Factory install resets the configuration. macOS Spotlight shortcuts depend on system settings.
